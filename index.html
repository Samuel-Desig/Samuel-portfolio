What's coming out of the oven

A short, rotating menu — small batches mean everything sells fresh, and some days something sells out by noon.

Country Sourdough

36-hour ferment, dark crackled crust, open crumb. Our first loaf, still the one people ask for by name.

Seeded Rye

Caraway and toasted sunflower folded through a dense rye base. Best a day after baking.

$10 · loaf

Morning Croissant

Laminated over three days, baked in small batches through the morning so there's always a warm one.
